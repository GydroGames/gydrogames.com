We're thrilled to announce that **Pickle Empire** is now live on Google Play's internal testing track. After months of iterating on the core idle-clicker loop and tuning progression with real player data, v1.3.0 is ready for wider testing.

## What is Pickle Empire?

Pickle Empire is an idle clicker game where you grow pickles, sell pickles, and build a pickle empire. Simple on the surface, deep underneath — with prestige layers, upgrade trees, and a progression system tuned by how real players actually advance through similar games.

## What We Changed in v1.3.0

- **AI-informed difficulty curve** — Early-game pacing was too slow. We used aggregated session data to identify the drop-off point and retuned the first 10 minutes.
- **Prestige system** — Players can now reset progress for permanent multipliers, unlocking a second gameplay loop.
- **Offline earnings** — You now earn pickles while the app is closed, scaled to a reasonable cap to preserve intent.
- **Performance pass** — Cut startup time by 40% on mid-range Android devices.

## How to Join Testing

Head to our [testing page](/testing) and sign in with your Google account. Once approved, you'll get a link to the Play Store internal test and Discord tester role access.

## What's Next

v1.4.0 will focus on visual polish and the first pass of seasonal events. Stay tuned via [our Discord](https://discord.gg/g7CPwwad).
